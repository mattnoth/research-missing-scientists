## Imbalance note

The **Mulder side has essentially gone quiet** since the plea; the only live "strange" thread is the systemic one (why a loaded-unregistered-rifle trespass case was dismissed and an attempted-escape count was charged that LASD says never happened), which is a criminal-justice-process question, not a national-security one, and the DA has declined to answer it.
