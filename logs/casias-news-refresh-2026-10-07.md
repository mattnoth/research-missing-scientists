## Headline findings

- **STATUS CHANGE — Missing → Deceased.** A hiker found skeletonized human remains on **2026-05-28** in the McGaffey Ridge area of Carson National Forest (reached via Rio Chiquito Road / Forest Road 437 off NM-518 in Talpa). NMSP was notified the same day; the Office of the Medical Investigator (OMI) positively identified Melissa Casias (NMSP later told LAmag the ID was by dental records) and NMSP published a release Saturday night **2026-05-30**. **A handgun was found alongside the remains.** [T1 NMSP release via Los Alamos Reporter reproduction; T3 Taos News / Santa Fe New Mexican; Confirmed]
- **Discovery was first disclosed by the Crime Junkie podcast on social media the evening of 2026-05-29 — a day before the NMSP release** (Taos News, SF New Mexican). This is a literal precursor-disclosure: a T7 venue published the find before the T1 agency. [Reported]
- **Autopsy (OMI; forensic pathologist Dr. Audra Kerwin), reported 2026-08-10:** cause = gunshot wound of the head; **manner = undetermined.** Bullet entered the bottom-right base of the skull, traveled right-to-left and upward, exited the left parietal; **no projectile or fragments recovered**; wound characterized as perimortem; range of fire could not be estimated (minimal soft tissue); advanced decomposition, left hand and left foot absent, "signs of predation"; remains leaning against stone in a rock depression with the left tibia/fibula, shoe and sock ~20 ft away; firearm near the body; preliminary toxicology "possible presence of cannabinoids," never confirmed. Manner certified undetermined "given the state of the body" and paucity of circumstantial information. ## Imbalance note

Coverage volume is heavily concentrated in **one reporter (Lauren Conlin, LAmag — 14 pieces, public records, NMSP exclusives)** and **one local paper (Taos News — Olivia Lewis / Geoffrey Plant)**; NMSP has effectively routed its substantive updates through these two channels plus Facebook. National outlets covered only the two inflection points (remains ID June 1; autopsy Aug 10–14) and otherwise relay the Daily Mail/McNally chain. The **Mondragon side** has institutional amplification (law firm, former PI's media access, "Justice for Melissa" page, Crime Junkie); the **Mark Casias side** speaks mainly through a court petition and Krista Misbach's Facebook, and is the subject of insinuation by a party now under a TRO. On evidentiary parity: the **homicide reading** (Mondragons; anonymous Daily Mail LE source; Hansen) and the **self-inflicted/voluntary reading** (Mark; Coffindaffer; Morgan's "atypical self-inflicted") both rest on interpretation of the same undetermined autopsy, the same gun-ownership fact, and the same wound geometry; neither has a documentary edge. The strange claims (DEW, UFO, serial killer, staged scene) and the mundane claims (tax-debt stress, lost clearance, marital issues) are each single-sourced at the point of origin. Repository prose should carry both at "Alleged/Reported" and avoid adopting "murder" or "suicide."
