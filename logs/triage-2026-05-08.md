## Section 4 — Methodology Footnote

**T4+ count:** grep for `T[4-8](?:/T[5-8])?` word-boundary matches. Counts inline tier tags only; does not count T3/T4 (lower bound = 3, excluded). casias has only 1 such tag (T3/T4 label, lower bound 3, not counted; one T4 only in CBS coverage note). grillmair has zero — all its inline tags are T1 or T3, reflecting excellent primary-source coverage.

**Reported/Alleged/Speculated count:** Includes section headers ("### Reported") and inline uses. This inflates counts in cases with long, well-structured tiering sections (loureiro: 28, chavez: 23). Loureiro's high R/A/S score reflects thorough documentation discipline, not poor sourcing — its total weakness score (46) is inflated relative to actual evidentiary weakness. Treat loureiro's ranking as a methodology artifact; it should not appear in union picks.

**Gap-marker count:** Matches lines (not words) containing any of ~15 phrases. reza's 5 is conservative — some withholding is implied rather than stated.

**Dead-link count:** 403 appears in hicks (2 T1 sources returned 403 on fetch), chavez (1 LAPD page, 1 paywalled Boomtown), maiwald (3: Legacy, SPIE, JPL pages with access notes), reza (2: since-removed SAR Facebook post, other). These represent actually inaccessible primary documentation.

**Latest date:** Month-YYYY pattern used; YYYY-MM-DD found only in hicks, maiwald, mccasland, reza. "April 2026" is the ceiling for most cases (cluster media burst). grillmair's April 29 arraignment is a future event at time of research (April 20, 2026).
