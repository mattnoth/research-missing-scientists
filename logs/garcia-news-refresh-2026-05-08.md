### B3. Domain investigation — KCNSC / NNSA nuclear-security context

The domain-investigation question is whether the KCNSC-Albuquerque-facility employment claim is plausible institutionally, and how the Kirtland AFB linkage surfaced via the anonymous source maps onto known KCNSC operations.

- **KCNSC overview (T1, kcnsc.doe.gov):** Kansas City National Security Campus is the NNSA / Honeywell FM&T-operated facility producing ~80% of non-nuclear components for U.S. nuclear weapons. **It is real, large, and has multiple satellite presences beyond its main Kansas City Missouri campus.** A New Mexico satellite presence in the Albuquerque area is institutionally plausible because:
  - KCNSC-produced components feed into final assembly that occurs at NNSA's Pantex (Texas) and Y-12 (Tennessee), but engineering, design, and testing collaboration with Sandia National Laboratories (Albuquerque) is well-documented.
  - Kirtland AFB hosts the Air Force Nuclear Weapons Center (AFNWC), which manages nuclear weapons sustainment and is geographically co-located with Sandia.
